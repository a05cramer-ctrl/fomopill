        How it works

        Four rules. Every number is Pip's running config.

        1. 1 min

          It reads fomo.

          Every minute Pip pulls fomo's newest Solana transactions. fomo co-signs every trade its users make, so Pip picks them out of the chain without ever logging into fomo.

        2. 5 + $250

          Two halves close.

          Every coin gets a capsule with two halves: fomo wallets and net money in. When 5 fomo wallets buy it inside 10 min and $250 more flows in than out, the capsule closes. That's a dose.

        3. 0.5 SOL

          Pip takes it.

          It buys with up to 10% of its wallet, at most 0.5 SOL, paid only from its own creator fees. Three positions at a time, never more.

        4. 1.5×

          Wins burn $PIP.

          Half comes off at 1.5×, the rest rides a 25% trailing stop. Hard stop at 0.7×, 12 h max. Profit buys $PIP and burns it on-chain.

        100% of $PIP creator fees go to Pip's wallet. That is the only money it ever trades with. Holders are never charged.

        FAQ

        Short answers.

        Is this fomo's token?

        No. fomo has no token. Pip The Trader reads public on-chain data and is not affiliated with fomo or pump.fun.

        Does it log into fomo or copy my trades?

        No. It never touches the fomo app or anyone's account. fomo co-signs every trade on Solana, so Pip reads them off the chain like anyone could.

        Does it read every fomo trade?

        It reads a fresh batch every minute, spread across the minute, which is enough to see where fomo's wallets are piling in. The page always shows how many trades it read.

        What does $PIP do?

        Its creator fees fund Pip's wallet, and every winning dose buys $PIP back and burns it. Holders are never charged for anything.

        What happens when a dose loses?

        Pip sells at its stop (0.7×) or after 12 h. The loss comes out of Pip's own wallet, and the trade stays on this page with its transaction.

        Why is the live read 5 minutes late for me?

        Once the holder gate is on, holders see it live. Show your prescription with a wallet that holds enough $PIP and the page switches to the live read.
